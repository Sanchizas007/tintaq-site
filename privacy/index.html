Privacy policy

The short version

Tintaq has no account and no server of its own, and it never sees your clients' data. Everything you enter about a client stays on your device. Only two things leave it, both described below: the purchase of Pro and, once per install, an anonymous Apple Ads code.

What the app stores, and where

Client names and contact details, answers to the health questions, photos of identity documents where you choose to take them, drawings on the body chart, signatures, and the generated PDFs. All of it lives in the app's own container on your device, protected by iOS file encryption and, when you leave it on, by Face ID or your passcode.

What leaves the device

What you send yourself: a PDF you share, print or email, and a backup file you save to Files or iCloud Drive. Those go where you send them, under Apple's terms or your mail provider's, not ours. Besides that, only the two things described further down.

What we collect

Nothing about your clients and nothing about how you use the app: no account, no usage analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising in the app. The one third party in the app is RevenueCat, which receives the purchase and ad-campaign data described below. The App Store privacy label lists exactly those two kinds of data, purchases and advertising data, neither linked to you and neither used for tracking.

Camera, photos and Face ID

The camera is used only to photograph an identity document for a form; the photo goes straight into the app and never into your camera roll. Photo library access is used only when you pick an existing image or your studio logo. Face ID is used only to unlock the app and to leave client mode; the biometric data never reaches the app — iOS only answers yes or no.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Pro is sold through the App Store, and your device decides whether you have it: Tintaq asks Apple whether this Apple ID owns the unlock and gets back yes or no. To follow subscriptions, the app also tells RevenueCat, a service that handles in-app purchases, about your purchases. RevenueCat receives your purchase receipt and transaction details from Apple, an anonymous app user ID that it generates, and the technical data that comes with any network request, such as your IP address, device model, system version, language and country. It never receives anything about your clients or anything you enter. We cannot tell who you are from this, and RevenueCat does not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites. RevenueCat's own policy, at revenuecat.com/privacy, describes how it handles that data. We never see your name, card or address.

Apple Ads attribution

Whether or not you came from an ad, the app asks Apple for an attribution code, which says nothing about who you are, and passes it to RevenueCat once per installation, together with the anonymous app user ID. If you installed Tintaq after tapping one of our ads in the App Store (Apple Ads), RevenueCat uses the code to ask Apple which campaign, ad group and keyword the ad belonged to, and keeps that with the same ID, so that we can see which ads lead to purchases. If you did not come from an ad, Apple reports no campaign. Tintaq does not read your advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not show the tracking-permission prompt, because nothing is combined with data from other companies' apps or websites. RevenueCat's policy, mentioned above, covers this data too.

Your clients' rights

Under the GDPR the studio is the controller of its clients' data. Tintaq is the tool, not a processor, because the data never reaches us. When a client asks to be erased, delete them in the app: the record, the signatures and the ID photo go with them.

Children

Tintaq is a tool for professionals and is not directed at children. A client under 18 is recorded with a guardian's signature where the law allows the procedure at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date below changes with it, and a material change will be mentioned in the app's release notes.
