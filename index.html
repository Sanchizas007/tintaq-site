Questions studios ask

Where is my clients' data stored?

On the device, in the app's own container, encrypted by iOS and locked behind Face ID. Nothing is sent anywhere — Tintaq has no server and no account.

What happens if I lose the phone?

Local-only storage means a lost phone is a lost archive, so Tintaq can write an encrypted backup file you keep in Files or iCloud Drive. Make one regularly.

Is a finger signature legally valid?

In most places an electronic signature is valid when you can show who signed, what they read and when. Tintaq stores all three in the PDF. Whether that satisfies your local rules is a question for a lawyer in your country.

Are the consent templates legally approved?

No. They are a well-written starting point, not legal advice. Anyone claiming an app gives you a compliant document for every country is selling you something. Read the template, change what needs changing, and check it locally.

Can a client under 18 sign?

Tintaq works out the age from the date of birth and switches on a guardian signature when the client is under 18. Whether you may tattoo them at all depends on your country and region.

Do I have to store a photo of the ID?

No. You can record that the document was checked, with its type and last digits, and store no image at all — the lighter option where keeping a scan is not allowed.

Does it work on an iPad?

Yes. Many studios hand the client an iPad to sign on, and the app is built for both.
